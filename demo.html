SwitchParentage A ploidy-preserving search move, step by step

Inferring allopolyploid histories is hard because most network rearrangements break the ploidy of the tips. SwitchParentage is the PhyNetPy implementation of SRPP from the MP-Allop-2 paper: detach a subnetwork, then reattach it so every leaf keeps the same subgenome count. This page runs one move on a toy Scenario D example and scores it the way the search does.

    What the scorer does
    
                    
    
                        A network is first unrolled into a MUL tree: the hybrid
                        clade is copied once per parent, giving subgenomes A (from a) and B
                        (from the other parent). Each gene tree is mapped into that MUL, and
                        the parsimony score is the number of extra lineages — coalescences that
                        had to happen deeper than the species tree allows.
                    
    
                    
    
                        On an edge, XL = lineages at the child − 1 − coalescences on the edge.
                        The search minimizes the sum over gene trees. Hill climbing accepts a
                        SwitchParentage proposal when that sum drops.
                    
    
                    
    
                        This toy: the starting parentage scores 9. After the
                        move, 2.
                    
    
                
    
            
    
        
    
    
    

    
    
        
    
            
    
                From the paper
                
    Why this move is the theoretical piece
    
                
    
                    MP-Allop-2 replaces PhyloNet's six-move kernel with one operation. The
                    PhyNetPy class is SwitchParentage; the paper calls it SRPP
                    (Sub-Network Redirect and Preserve Ploidy).
                
    
            
    
            
    
                
    
                    
    Ploidy is an invariant
    
                    
    
                        After disconnect, every leaf in the detached subnetwork drops by at
                        least 1. Reattach only uses edges whose p(e) can restore
                        the recorded target, so the proposal is valid by construction — no
                        reject-and-retry on illegal tips.
                    
    
                
    
                
    
                    
    The space is reachable
    
                    
    
                        Any two valid networks with the same leaf ploidy assignment can be
                        transformed into each other by a finite sequence of SRPP moves. The
                        paper also shows invertibility, so the move is usable in MCMC as well
                        as hill climbing.
                    
    
                
    
                
    
                    
    Cost is linear
    
                    
    
                        CalculatePloidy is a top-down pass, O(|V| + |E|). That is
                        the same order as the cycle-avoidance work the old kernel already did,
                        except here the work produces a legal network instead of a discarded one.
